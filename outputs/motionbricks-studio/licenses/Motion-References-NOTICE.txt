MotionBricks Studio Ti — non-dance reference revisions (2026-11-02)

These are adapted reference motions, not wholly independent motion capture
by MASATAKA. Do not remove the following credits when distributing the data.
The per-clip provenance and CATALOG.csv identify sources, intervals, SHA-256,
licenses and modifications. No NVIDIA/Bones RigPlay sample animation was an
input to this revision. Existing restricted samples are a separate collection.

The resulting anatomical reconstruction is authored animation, not measured
human mocap; its quality remains under review.

Modifications by MotionBricks Studio Ti: source interval selection, 30 Hz
resampling, BVH-axis and neutral-pose calibration, bent-frame knee-axis
calibration, same-take foot/scale calibration, transfer to SOMA proportions,
skinned-sole contact fitting, torso-width adjustment and relaxed finger poses.
Some clips additionally use heading/path changes, joined takes, neck-only
extraction/retiming, abdominal hand placement, obstacle-height adaptation or
shared chair/passage placement. See each clip's provenance for details.
